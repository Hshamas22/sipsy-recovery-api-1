## 4️⃣ "Why is Mezcal Smoky?"
**Format:** Educational / Visual explanation  
**Vibe:** Expert storyteller, curiosity-driven

### Script:
```
[OPEN: Hala at Sipsy, holding a mezcal bottle]

HALA (V.O.): "Unlike tequila—which is steamed in ovens—mezcal is traditionally roasted in underground pits."

[Show the pit, flames, earth]

HALA: "They throw the agave heart INTO THE GROUND with hot rocks and wood smoke. It cooks for like... days."

[Show agave being cooked]

HALA: "That smoke? That's not a flaw. That's the POINT. That's flavor."

HALA: "So when someone says 'mezcal tastes smoky'—they're tasting the method. They're tasting tradition."

[Text overlay: "Smoke = Quality", "Tradition = Flavor"]

[Quick shot of 2-3 mezcal bottles at different smoke levels]

HALA: "Want to start exploring? Don't go for the smokiest. Go for the one that makes you curious."

[Text: "Link in bio for beginner mezcals"]
```

**Why It Works:**
- Answers a common question (high search volume)
- Visual explanation (TikTok gold)
- Educational without being boring (adds value)
- Makes product make sense (removes intimidation)
- Ends with a warm recommendation (not salesy)
- Shareable: "oh NOW I get it"
