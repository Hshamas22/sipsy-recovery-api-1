## 🚀 How to Use These Templates

1. **In Shopify Admin:** Apps & Sales Channels → Email
2. **Create Campaign**
3. **Select segment** (e.g., "Customers tagged: tequila")
4. **Click "Design email"**
5. **Use this guide to write copy** (or copy-paste exactly)
6. **Use Shopify's drag-and-drop** to format

## Copy Tips for Maximum Conversion

✅ **DO:**
- Use their first name ("Hi Sarah" not "Hi Customer")
- Personalize by segment ("New tequila for tequila lovers")
- Use emojis (breaks up text, increases engagement)
- Keep it short (2-3 paragraphs max)
- Include 1 clear CTA ("SHOP NOW")
- Reference their past purchases ("You loved Fortaleza...")

❌ **DON'T:**
- Write too long (people scan emails)
- Use "You're our favorite customer" if not true
- Push too many products (pick 1-2 max)
- Over-discount (kills brand value)
- Spam weekly (2-3x per week max)
