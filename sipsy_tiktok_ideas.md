# Sipsy TikTok Ideas - 20 Content Concepts

## 1. **"Why I Chose Premium Over Viral"**
**Main Topic:** Founder's decision to prioritize quality over trends
**Key Talking Points:**
- The temptation to chase viral trends vs. staying authentic to Sipsy's mission
- How saying "no" to trends actually builds a more loyal community
- The long-term value of premium positioning over quick wins

**Tone/Style:** Reflective, slightly vulnerable, expert conviction
**Why It Works:** Speaks to audience who values authenticity and is tired of influencer culture. Shows founder's integrity and business philosophy.
