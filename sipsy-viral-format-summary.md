## Why These Formats Work

✅ **Tier lists & rankings** - High debate in comments, rewatchable  
✅ **POV videos** - Puts viewers in the scenario, high engagement  
✅ **Taste tests/blind comparisons** - Inherently suspenseful, trust-building  
✅ **Educational content** - Saved/shared, positions Sipsy as expert  
✅ **Hacks/Tips** - Bookmarked utility content, highly shareable  
✅ **ASMR/Aesthetic** - Massive reach, low-commitment background watching  
✅ **Price comparisons** - Massive engagement, value-conscious viewers  
✅ **Problem/Solution** - High relatability, self-deprecating humor shares  
✅ **Challenges** - Drives engagement (people try themselves)  
✅ **Trending audio twist** - Algorithm loves trending sounds, emotional resonance  
✅ **Money/Economics** - Finance content huge following, practical info  
✅ **Reactions** - Humor + education, comments = pronunciation engagement  
✅ **Edutainment** - Authority building, viewers feel smart  
✅ **Plot twist** - Must-watch-to-end format, subverts expectations  
✅ **Expert secrets** - Insider knowledge triggers curiosity  
✅ **Before/After** - Satisfying transformation, aspirational  
✅ **Niche discovery** - Shareable gems, builds curator credibility  
✅ **Nostalgia** - Emotional storytelling, community shares memories  
✅ **Comparative research** - Educational + surprising, debate comments  
✅ **Mini-documentary** - Hypnotic process videos, repeat views
