# Sipsy Email Marketing Strategy
**Date:** March 20, 2026  
**Focus:** High-ROI customer segmentation & targeted email campaigns

---

## Executive Summary

Instead of paid ads ($100+ CAC), build revenue through **email segmentation + targeted campaigns** (near $0 CAC). 

**Your data:**
- 100+ customers with rich purchase history
- Diverse product mix (Tequila, Whiskey, Champagne, Cocktail Sets, Gift Boxes)
- Gift purchases clearly identifiable (those who buy "Gift Box")
- Perfect for segment-based emails

**ROI:** Email converts at 2-5% vs. ads at 0.5-1%, so $0 CAC beats $100 CAC every time.

## Customer Segments to Build

### 1. **Gift Buyers** 🎁
**Identify:** Customers who ordered "Gift Box" or "Gift Wrapping"

**Current Product Mix:**
- Veuve Clicquot Champagne Yellow Arrow Gift Box
- Whiskey Sidecar Cocktail Set - 6-8 Servings
- Gift Box (Fits one bottle)
- Veuve Clicquot Ice Jacket

**Email Strategy:**
- **Campaign 1:** "Think of Someone Special" (seasonal: holidays, birthdays)
  - Trigger: 30 days after gift purchase
  - Subject: "Your recipient loved their gift... here's something for YOU"
  - Offer: $15 off their own order (loyalty for the gifter)
  
- **Campaign 2:** Holiday Gift Guide (Oct-Nov)
  - Segment: Past gift buyers only
  - Subject: "Curated gifts for the spirits lover in your life"
  - Content: Gift sets, cocktail kits, premium bottles
  - CTA: Free gift wrapping included
  
- **Campaign 3:** Birthday/Anniversary Campaigns (if Smile.io tracks this)
  - Trigger: Customer birthday (30 days before)
  - Subject: "A gift for YOU this month"
  - Offer: Free gift wrapping or $20 off

**Expected Conversion:** 8-12% (gifts are high-intent purchases)
**Estimated Revenue:** 50 gift buyers × $150 avg × 10% = $750/month

## Required Email Platform Features

**You need an ESP (Email Service Provider) with:**
- ✅ Segmentation/tagging
- ✅ Automated workflows/triggers
- ✅ Dynamic content (personalization)
- ✅ A/B testing
- ✅ Shopify integration

**Recommended Platforms:**
1. **Klaviyo** (best for ecommerce)
   - Built-in Shopify sync
   - Visual automation builder
   - Excellent segmentation
   - $0-300/month depending on list size
   
2. **Omnisend**
   - SMS + Email
   - Good automation
   - $0-300/month
   
3. **Mailchimp**
   - Basic (free up to 500 contacts)
   - Limited automation
   - Good for starting out

**DO NOT USE:** Shopify's basic email (too limited for segmentation)

## Key Success Factors

✅ **Do This:**
- Segment by actual purchase history (not guesses)
- Personalize subject lines ("New tequila for tequila lovers")
- Test send times (spirits buyers may open email differently than others)
- Track ROI per segment (some will be better than others)
- Send consistently (weekly > sporadic)

❌ **Don't Do This:**
- Send same email to all segments (waste of valuable data)
- Blast promotions too often (damages brand)
- Ignore metrics (you can't improve what you don't measure)
- Buy email lists (illegal for spirits in many states)
- Go weeks without emailing (customers forget you exist)
