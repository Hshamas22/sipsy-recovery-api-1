## Create These 5 Segments in Shopify Email (2 minutes)

**Path:** Shopify Admin → Apps & Sales Channels → Email → Audiences

### SEGMENT 1: All Subscribers
1. Click "Create Audience"
2. Name: `All Subscribers`
3. Filter: **No filters** (leave blank = everyone)
4. Expected count: **29,099**
5. Click **Save**
